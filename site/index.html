Stateful vs. stateless

This model remembers permitted flow tuples and matches their replies. Cisco ACL established only checks TCP ACK/RST flags; it does not maintain a session table. Neither is a full stateful firewall here: no TCP sequence validation, teardown or idle expiration is implemented.

Security boundaries

Same-VLAN traffic bypasses the gateway. NAT is not a firewall and does not guarantee topology secrecy. HTTPS may carry exfiltration or malware. This is routed segmentation, not a physical air gap. Industrial DPI, USB controls and hardware data diodes remain production recommendations.
